I'm Akhil, a data center connectivity engineer based in Irving, TX. Since January 2023 I've worked on Meta hyperscale data center deployments, testing, validating, and documenting the fiber that connects everything inside these buildings.

I started this site for two reasons. I wanted one place where my resume, projects, and writing live together. And a lot of what I learn in the field never gets written down anywhere, so this is where I'll put it.

## What I'll write about

- **Data center & fiber:** field lessons from deployments, testing, and turnover
- **Networking & certifications:** CCNA notes, the ANSI certification I'm working on, and lab work
- **Projects:** tools I build, like my fiber link health monitor
- **Career:** what I'm learning as I grow toward systems and connectivity engineering
- **Life & updates:** everything else

If any of that sounds interesting, check back, or [connect with me on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akhil-inturi-b5ba2619a/).
